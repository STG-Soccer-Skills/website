Frequently Asked Questions

Have more questions about STG Unlimited?
Here are answers to the ones we hear most from parents.

Is there a long-term contract or can we cancel?
All memberships are month-to-month. There’s no long-term contract. If you need to pause or cancel, just let us know at least 7 days before your next billing date and we’ll take care of it.
How many sessions can my player attend each week?
With STG Unlimited, players can attend up to one structured training session per day, Monday–Friday, plus any eligible pick-up games and monthly skills clinics. Most families attend 2–3 days per week depending on their schedule and season.
What’s included in the $99 player evaluation?
The evaluation is a one-time 60 minute session where we assess your player’s technical skills, athletic movement, and game understanding. From there we place them in the right age/skill group (Gold or Silver) and build a recommended training plan.
What’s the difference between Gold and Silver groups?
Gold groups are for players who are a bit further along in their development and ready for a faster pace. Silver groups emphasize fundamentals and confidence-building. Both groups follow the same curriculum themes each week — just at different speeds and complexity levels. It is possible to move from one group to another given improvement over time.
Are leagues included in the Unlimited membership?
No, leagues are separate programs. STG Unlimited covers trainings, speed & agility, pick-up games, and monthly skills clinics. Indoor leagues have their own registration and schedule but pair really well with the Unlimited Academy.
