What is Mini Movers?

Play, move and discover the ball

Mini Movers uses imaginative play, simple ball activities and guided movement games, all indoors. Parents are welcome on the field and encouraged to join in. No experience needed.

  • Gross motor skills
  • Early coordination and ball familiarity
  • Confidence in group settings
  • Social awareness and interaction
  • Enjoyment of movement and play
Who it's for

A great fit for

  • Toddlers ages 1–3 starting to explore movement
  • Shy, cautious or slow-to-warm-up kids
  • High-energy toddlers
  • Kids building balance, coordination and body awareness
  • Kids who do better with a parent nearby
  • Toddlers new to structured activities
  • Parents who want a safe, indoor activity
60-minute class

What a class looks like

  1. Arrival & warm welcome (5–10 min). Kids explore the space, greet coaches and ease into low-pressure play.
  2. Movement warm-up games (10–15 min). Running, jumping, balance games and chasing cones.
  3. Ball discovery (15–20 min). Rolling, tapping, gentle dribbling and kicking toward big targets.
  4. Parent & child activity (10–15 min). Partner games that build bonding and help hesitant kids.
  5. Cool down & high-fives (5 min). A calm activity, a stretch or breathing moment, and high-fives.

Is it okay if my child is shy or nervous?

Yes. Kids move at their own pace, and parents can stay close until their child feels comfortable.

Do I stay with my child?

Yes. Parents are on the field or sideline, depending on your child's comfort and the activity.

What should my child wear and bring?

Comfortable athletic clothes, athletic shoes and a water bottle. A favorite small ball is optional. No special gear needed.

What if we miss a session?

Make-ups aren't guaranteed, but we try to be flexible if you let us know ahead of time.

Is this okay if my child has never taken a class?

Yes. Mini Movers is designed for first-timers.
