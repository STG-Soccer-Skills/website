About the league

First touches, small-sided fun

Lil Kickers is 3v3 indoor soccer built for our youngest players: lots of touches, small-sided play and a referee at every game. Games are on Fridays, starting Friday, November 6. Exact game times are posted in the weekly schedule.

Players can sign up on their own and we'll place them on a balanced team (friend and sibling requests welcome), or you can register a whole team. We provide a coach for any team that needs one.
