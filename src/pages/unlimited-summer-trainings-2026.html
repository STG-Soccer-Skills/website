Frequently asked questions

What if we can't make every session?

No problem. With multiple sessions offered each day, you've got built-in flexibility to fit the schedule that works for your family. Come as often as you can — every session counts.

Is this worth it for a newer player?

Absolutely. Our sessions are structured for skill development at every level. Beginners build fundamentals fast in a high-rep environment. Advanced players sharpen what they already have.

When can I start training with the discounted $399 training program?

The $399/month membership starts from date of purchase and automatically renews each month.

Why indoor in the summer?

Indoor soccer means smaller spaces, faster decisions, and more touches per hour than outdoor. It's one of the best-kept secrets in player development — and in St. George's summer heat, it's also a lot more comfortable.

Can I sign up for just one month?

Yes! You can register for June, July, or both. Either way, lock in before May 21st to get the $299 early bird rate.

What if I already have the Elite Unlimited Membership?

Congratulations! You can come to all sessions without any additional cost.
