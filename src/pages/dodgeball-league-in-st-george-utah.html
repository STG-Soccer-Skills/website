Common questions

Season, explained

When does the season start?

Thursday, September 10. From there you'll play eight weeks of regular-season games, capped off by a championship final.

How much is it, and how is it billed?

$799 per team, paid one time for the whole season. It's not a subscription — one entry fee covers all eight weeks plus the final.

How many games do we get?

Eight weeks of regular-season matchups, then the final for teams that qualify. Every game counts toward your standing and seeding.

What if we're short players one week?

Subs are welcome and your roster holds up to 12, so build in depth. As long as you can field eight, you play.

What skill level is this for?

Competitive but welcoming — if you can throw, you can play. Flag your level when you register so we can keep matchups fair.
