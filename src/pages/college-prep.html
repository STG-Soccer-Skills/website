FAQ

College Prep questions

Who is this for?

High school players, roughly ages 14 to 18, who are serious about playing in college. Membership is invite-only.

Is this the same as other STG trainings?

No. College Prep trainings are separate from all other STG programs and sessions.

What do players work on?

Guided, hands-on work on getting recruited, getting film (highlight film and game film), reaching out to college coaches, private trainings, and what playing college soccer is actually like.

Who runs it?

Sawyer Heaton, plus guest coaches with real college connections.

What does it cost, and when does it meet?

Membership is by invitation. This page does not list a price or a schedule.
