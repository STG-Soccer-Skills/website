Privacy Policy

Last updated October 12, 2026

STG Soccer Skills ("STG Soccer Skills," "we," "us" or "our") runs the indoor soccer facility at 3087 S River Rd, St. George, UT 84790 and the website stgsoccerskills.com. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have, including for text messages.

Information we collect

We collect information you give us when you register for a league, training, camp, membership or party, book field time, sign up for our text messages or emails, fill out a form, or contact us. This may include:

  • Your name, email address, mobile phone number and mailing address
  • Player information you provide for registration, such as a player's name, age or birth year, team and emergency contact
  • Booking and registration details, such as the program, dates and team name
  • Messages you send us

Payments are handled by our payment processors. We do not store full credit or debit card numbers on our website.

Like most websites, our site and the services it uses may automatically receive basic technical information such as your browser type, device and pages visited.

How we use your information

  • To process registrations, bookings, memberships and payments
  • To send schedules, game and field updates, reminders, confirmations and other service messages
  • To answer your questions and provide customer support
  • To send news, events and offers, only if you have opted in to receive them
  • To run, secure and improve our programs and website, and to meet legal requirements

Text messages (SMS)

If you give us your mobile number and opt in, STG Soccer Skills may send you text messages. Depending on what you agree to, these can include registration and booking confirmations, schedules, game and field changes, reminders, account and billing notices, customer support replies, and (only with separate consent) promotional messages about our programs and events.

  • Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase.
  • Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to any message to opt out at any time. You'll receive one final message confirming you've been unsubscribed.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact us at 844-608-1116 or book@stgsoccerskills.com.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing with subcontractors in support services, such as customer service and message delivery, is permitted. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers that help us run our business, such as our customer messaging and booking platform, payment processors, and email, calendar and website hosting providers. They may use your information only to provide services to us.
  • With league officials, coaches or referees when needed to run a program you joined
  • When required by law, or to protect the safety, rights or property of our players, customers, staff or others
  • In connection with a sale or transfer of our business, subject to this policy

Cookies and third-party content

Our website does not run its own advertising or tracking cookies. Some pages include content from other services, such as registration and contact forms, a reviews widget, Google Calendar, Google Maps, Google Fonts and embedded videos. Those services may set their own cookies and collect technical information under their own privacy policies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Children's privacy

Many of our programs are for youth players, but registrations are completed by a parent or guardian. Our website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

How we protect and keep information

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards, such as encrypted connections and limited staff access, to protect your information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes described here, such as running your registration, keeping business and tax records, and meeting legal requirements.

Your choices

  • Text messages: reply STOP to opt out, or HELP for help.
  • Email: use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email.
  • Access, correction or deletion: email book@stgsoccerskills.com or call 844-608-1116 and we will respond within a reasonable time, subject to any records we must keep by law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed. Continued use of our website or services after an update means you accept the updated policy.
