Frequently Asked Questions

My child has never played before. Is that okay?

Absolutely. This program is specifically designed for beginners and young players who are just getting started.

Is this program competitive?

The focus is primarily on fun, learning, confidence, and development in a positive environment. Focusing on making soccer fun at a young age helps kids become competitive and stick with it as they develop a love for the game.

What should my child bring?

Players should bring athletic clothes, a water bottle, shin guards if required, and a soccer ball if requested by the program. Wear tennis or indoor shoes.

What if my child is shy or nervous?

That’s very common. Our coaches are experienced in helping young players feel comfortable, included, and confident.
