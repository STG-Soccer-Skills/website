What is Junior Kickers?

The next step up

Junior Kickers is a foundational program for kids ages 4–5 who are ready for more structure and instruction. Every session mixes age-appropriate skills, movement and coordination games, listening and focus activities, and small-sided games.

No soccer experience needed.

Who it's for

A great fit for

  • Kids who love running, kicking and playing
  • Shy kids or those building confidence in groups
  • High-energy kids who thrive with active games
  • Kids learning to follow directions and focus
  • Kids developing teamwork and social skills
  • New players looking for a fun first experience
  • Families wanting an indoor, structured program
60-minute class

What a class looks like

  1. Warm-up & movement games (10 min).
  2. Skill introduction (10–15 min). Dribbling, passing, ball control or striking.
  3. Guided skill activities (10–15 min). Challenges, obstacle courses and partner games.
  4. Small-sided games (15 min).
  5. Wrap-up & high-fives (5 min).

Does my child need experience?

No. Junior Kickers is built for beginners.

Do I drop off or stay?

Parents are welcome to watch from the sideline. Many families drop off with a parent nearby.

What if my child has trouble focusing or listening?

Our coaches use short, engaging activities and gentle redirection. We don't expect perfect behavior.

What should we bring?

Athletic clothes, sneakers or indoor soccer shoes and a water bottle. Shin guards are optional.

What if we miss a class?

Make-ups aren't guaranteed, but we try to be flexible if you let us know ahead of time.

Is it competitive?

No. It's training-focused, with no scores or standings.
