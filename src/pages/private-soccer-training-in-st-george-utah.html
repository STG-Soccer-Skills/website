How it works

Three steps to your first session

  1. Choose a membership. Private sessions require an active STG membership (Core, Development or Elite Unlimited).
  2. Submit a request. Tell us your player's age, position and goals so we can match a coach and time.
  3. Train. Work through a personalized plan that adjusts as your player improves.
Session details

What every session covers

  • 60 minutes of focused training built around position, age and goals
  • First touch, dribbling, striking, passing, decision-making and speed of play
  • Ongoing communication between coach, player and parent

FAQ

Private training questions

Do I need a membership for private sessions?

Yes. Private sessions are available to active Core, Development and Elite Unlimited members.

What ages is private training best for?

Private sessions typically work best for players ages 7 and up.

Can I request a specific coach?

Yes. We'll do our best to match your preferred coach, depending on availability.

How many private sessions should my player do?

Most athletes benefit from one private session per week.

Is there a sibling discount?

Yes. Additional siblings get 50% off on the Development and Elite Unlimited plans.
