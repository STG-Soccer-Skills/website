FAQ

Academy questions

Is this a school?

No. STG Soccer Academy is a private educational services program. Students are homeschoolers, and parents keep legal responsibility for their education. We help families through withdrawing from their current school.

Can my child still play for the local school team?

Yes. Utah's HB 324 lets homeschool students try out for public school sports.

My child has an IEP or 504 plan. Can they attend?

Yes. We review existing documentation and tailor instruction, but we don't write IEPs or 504 plans.

How involved do parents need to be?

Involvement is welcome but not required during program hours. There's no busy work, only meaningful projects.

Can we travel during the year?

Yes, with advance notice to your coach.

Will this help my child play college soccer?

The program is built to prepare athletes for NCAA eligibility and recruiting.

Ready to raise a champion?

Class size is capped at 10 students per coach. Questions? Call or text 844-608-1116.
