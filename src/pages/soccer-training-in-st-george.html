Soccer Programs & Training in St. George

STG Soccer Skills offers a full range of soccer training in St. George for players of every age and level. From indoor leagues to private coaching and specialty programs, you can build your game in a fun, supportive, and professional environment.

Indoor Adult Soccer Leagues

Fast-paced, competitive indoor leagues for adults who want to stay fit, compete, and have fun. Join a team with friends or sign up as a free agent and we’ll help place you.

Youth Indoor Soccer Leagues

Development-focused leagues for kids and teens that combine real game experience with a positive, encouraging atmosphere. Perfect for young players who love to play.

1-on-1 Private Soccer Training

Personalized training sessions focused on footwork, ball control, passing, shooting, and game awareness. Ideal for players who want faster improvement and individual attention.

Academy Membership Training

A structured, long-term development program for committed players who want consistent, high-level training. Build technical skills, decision-making, and confidence over time.

Birthday Parties & Events

Host an unforgettable soccer birthday party on our indoor field. Fun games, music, and a safe, energetic environment for kids to celebrate and play.

Indoor Field Reservations

Reserve our indoor turf for team practices, small groups, or private events. Perfect for coaches, clubs, and families who need reliable indoor space in St. George.

Toddler Training (Ages 1–3)

Fun, movement-based sessions that introduce soccer in a playful way. We focus on coordination, balance, and confidence in a low-pressure setting.

Junior Training (Ages 4–5)

Age-appropriate drills, games, and activities that teach basic soccer skills while keeping things exciting and enjoyable for young players.

Youth Training (Ages 6–18)

Training for beginners through advanced players. Sessions include technical work, small-sided games, and situational play to help players perform better in real matches.

Speed & Agility Training

Improve speed, quickness, balance, and overall athleticism. These sessions are great for players who want an edge in explosiveness and conditioning.

Pickup Soccer Games

Open-play sessions where players can jump in, play freely, and enjoy the game in a relaxed environment. A perfect way to stay active between leagues or training cycles.
