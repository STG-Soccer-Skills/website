FAQ

Membership questions

Are there contracts?

No. All memberships are month-to-month. To pause or cancel, give notice at least 7 days before your next billing date.

How often can Unlimited players train?

Unlimited players can attend one structured session per weekday, plus eligible pickup games and clinics. Most families come 2–3 days a week.

What is the player evaluation?

A one-time, 60-minute assessment of technical skill, athletic movement and game understanding. We use it to place your player in a Gold or Silver group.

What's the difference between Gold and Silver?

Gold is for players ready for a faster pace. Silver focuses on fundamentals and confidence. Both follow the same weekly themes, and players can move between groups as they improve.

Are leagues included?

No. Leagues are separate from memberships and have their own registration.
